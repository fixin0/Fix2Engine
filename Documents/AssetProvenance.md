## Register new assets

For each new asset, record its repository path, author/rightsholder, original URL,
exact license/version or permission record, modifications, required attribution,
and intended distribution. If the author is a contributor, obtain permission
covering the project's intended use; do not assume the code's MIT license applies
to an externally sourced asset.
